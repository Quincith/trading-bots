**Idea:** Build the opening range from the first 60 minutes instead of the first 30, so a breakout has to clear a bigger, more meaningful high or low.

**Why:** The first half hour after the market opens is noisy. Prices poke above or below a small range and then snap back, which hits the stop. Waiting a full hour gives a wider range. A move past it is more likely to be a real move that lasts to the end of the session. This is a common, simple choice, not a number tuned to these months.

**What Claude noticed:** Almost every loss ends at the stop: 366 trades were stopped out at an average of -0.32%. The 361 trades held to the end of the session averaged +0.34%. Most losers were opened right after the 30-minute range closed, at 07:30-07:40 or 08:30-08:40. By rough count that is about two-thirds of the roughly 440 listed losers. Losses also cluster in jumpy periods: March 2020 had about 35 stop-outs and Sep-Nov 2022 had over 80. That points to false breakouts out of a range too narrow to mean much. Caveat: the list shows only one winner (opened 08:35 and held to session end), so I cannot check entry times for winners.

**Change:**

- `forex.range_minutes`: 30 → 60 (The opening range is the high and low of this many minutes after the open)
