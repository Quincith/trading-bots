**Idea:** Sell every trade after 20 days instead of 30, because the big wins all came early and the late days mostly brought slow slides into the safety exit.

**Why:** Insider buying seems to give a push in the first couple of weeks. Every trade that reached its +20% goal did it within 17 days. After about three weeks, trades mostly drifted, and several slid down to the -10% exit. Selling at day 20 keeps all the early winners. It frees our money sooner and avoids some of those slow late losses. We don't know each trade's price on day 20, so the next test will show if this really helps.

**What Claude noticed:** Big losses all come from the 10% safety exit. 13 stopped trades averaged -9.6%. The 5 profit-goal trades averaged +20.1%, and the 25 time-limit trades averaged only +1.5%. All 5 profit-goal hits came within 17 days (days 5, 6, 14, 17, 17). Six of the 13 stops came late, on days 19 to 30, after two-plus weeks of drifting down: MAGN d19, NEXT d19, ASAN d21, MKZR d21, EMN d27 and SFD d30. Four of those (ASAN, MKZR, EMN, SFD) came after day 20 and together lost about -33%. The extra days past day 20 never produced a profit-goal hit. Insider count, score and dollar size showed no clean split between winners and losers.

**Change:**
