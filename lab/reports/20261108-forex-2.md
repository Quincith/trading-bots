# Lab experiment 20261108-forex-2: Forex

**❌ Rejected: Better on the unseen months; Still better with double fees and slip; Not a lucky number; Also not worse on the study months**

**Idea:** Close open trades at 17:00 instead of 16:00, so the trades that are already working get one more hour to grow.

**Why:** This strategy wins only when a trade survives the day; the forced close is where all its profit comes from. Losers usually hit their stop early, before the close, so an extra hour changes little for them. Winners get more time to move in our favour. This is one simple idea, not a finely tuned number, so it should work on unseen months. It differs from the rejected idea, which changed the entry, not the exit.

**What Claude noticed:** Every trade ends one of two ways. 366 trades hit the stop and lost, averaging -0.32%. The other 361 were still open at the 16:00 close and averaged +0.34%. All the profit comes from these trades that survive to the close. About 284 of them were winners. The roughly 77 that closed red were mostly tiny losses near -0.1%, like -0.02 or -0.08. Losing trades are not clustered at late entry times: only about 25 of 443 opened more than 2.5 hours after the open. That rules out a tighter entry window. The losses are spread across all pairs and both buy and sell. They bunch in choppy stretches such as March 2020 and mid-to-late 2022. None of the menu filters can pick those out from this data. One caveat: the trade list shows only one winner (+1.32%, closed at the session end). So the winners' exit times can't be checked directly.

**Change:**

- `forex.exit_hour`: 16 → 17 (Close any open trade at this hour (local time of that market))

Study months: Nov 1, 2018 to Aug 20, 2023. Unseen months: Aug 20, 2023 to Nov 1, 2026. Each replay starts with $100.00, fees and price slip included.
