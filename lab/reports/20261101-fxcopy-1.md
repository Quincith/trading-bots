# Lab experiment 20261101-fxcopy-1: Fxcopy

**❌ Rejected: Beats the yardstick (do nothing (keep the cash))**

**Idea:** Only copy the hedge funds when their bet got bigger since the week before, not when it is shrinking.

**Why:** A big bet that is getting smaller often means the funds are starting to back out. Buying then means joining just as they leave. In these months, the shrinking-bet trades lost about three times more than they won. Skipping them drops mostly losers (13 of 18). It keeps 20 of the 25 winners. It is one on/off switch, not a tuned number, so it should hold up better on new months.

**What Claude noticed:** Trades opened when the hedge funds' bet had shrunk since the week before did badly. That was 18 trades: 13 losers and 5 winners. Together they lost about -7.3%, which is nearly the whole -7.4% total. Trades where the bet had grown since the week before were 55 trades: 35 losers and 20 winners. Together they came out roughly flat (about 0%). Raising the size threshold looks wrong. Of the 16 trades with a net bet of 20% or more, 12 were losers, so very large bets did not help.

**Change:**

- `forex.need_growing`: 0 → 1 (1 = only copy a bet that grew since the week before (0 = off))

Study months: Nov 1, 2018 to Aug 20, 2023. Unseen months: Aug 20, 2023 to Nov 1, 2026. Each replay starts with $100.00, fees and price slip included.

| | Now | With the change |
|---|---|---|
| Study months | -7.4% | -0.5% |
| **Unseen months** | **-7.9%** | **-6.8%** |
| Unseen, fees and slip doubled | -15.9% | -14.2% |
| Worst drop from a high (unseen) | -13.2% | -12.8% |
| Trades (unseen) | 32 | 31 |
| Do nothing (keep the cash) (unseen) | +0.0% | |

## Checks

- ✅ **Better on the unseen months**: -6.8% with the change vs -7.9% now (needs at least 1 point better)
- ✅ **Steady month to month**: not worse in 4 of 5 slices (needs 4)
- ✅ **Still better with double fees and slip**: -14.2% vs -15.9% with costs doubled
- ✅ **Not a lucky number**: nothing to nudge (a filter was switched off)
- ❌ **Beats the yardstick (do nothing (keep the cash))**: -6.8% vs +0.0% on the unseen months
- ✅ **Enough trades to judge**: 31 trades on the unseen months (needs 12)
- ✅ **No deeper losing streak**: worst drop -12.8% vs -13.2% now
- ✅ **Also not worse on the study months**: -0.5% vs -7.4% on the months Claude looked at
