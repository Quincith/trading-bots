# Lab experiment 20261108-copycat-2: Copycat

**❌ Rejected: Steady month to month; Not a lucky number; Also not worse on the study months**

**Idea:** Give each trade more room by moving the safety exit from 10% to 15% below our entry price.

**Why:** Stocks that insiders buy are often small and jumpy, so a 10% dip can be ordinary noise. Many of our stop-outs came late, near the 30-day sell date. With more room, some of those trades would have reached the time limit and been sold, likely for a smaller loss or a small gain. That beats locking in the full 10% loss. The trade-off is that real failures will lose a bit more each time.

**What Claude noticed:** No entry setting separates winners from losers. Single-insider trades went 6 wins and 5 losses. Trades with score under 3 went 4 wins and 4 losses, and the 4 wins were bigger. Splitting by dollars bought (under or over $1M) nets about zero either way. The damage comes from the safety exit. 13 stop exits averaged -9.62%, about -125 points in total. The 5 target hits added about +100, and the 25 time-limit trades averaged only +1.53%. Many stops look like normal swings rather than broken stories. 6 of the 13 stops (ASAN, MAGN, EMN, SFD, NEXT, MKZR) came on day 19 or later, close to the 30-day sell date. Trades that dodged the stop drifted slightly positive on average.

**Change:**

- `copycat.stop_pct`: 10 → 15 (Safety exit this % below our entry)

Study months: Mar 31, 2025 to Nov 5, 2025. Unseen months: Nov 5, 2025 to Mar 31, 2026. Each replay starts with $100.00, fees and price slip included.

## Checks

- ❌ **Steady month to month**: not worse in 2 of 5 slices (needs 4)
- ✅ **Still better with double fees and slip**: +6.3% vs +3.7% with costs doubled
- ❌ **Not a lucky number**: nudged one step either way: +2.4%, +4.5% vs +4.9% now

- ✅ **No deeper losing streak**: worst drop -7.2% vs -9.7% now
- ❌ **Also not worse on the study months**: -5.6% vs -2.8% on the months Claude looked at
