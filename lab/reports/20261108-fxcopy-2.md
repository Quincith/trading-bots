# Lab experiment 20261108-fxcopy-2: Fxcopy

**❌ Rejected: Steady month to month; Beats the yardstick (do nothing (keep the cash))**

**Idea:** Give each trade more room by moving the safety stop from 3 to 4.5 typical daily swings away from the entry price.

**Why:** This strategy follows slow weekly data from big funds. Their bets often pay off over weeks or months. Right now normal day-to-day wobbles hit the stop before the idea has time to work. A wider stop lets more trades reach the planned exit, which has been profitable. Each stop-out still costs about the same, because the trade size shrinks to match the wider stop. The catch: winners shrink a little too. The bet is that fewer early stop-outs make up for that.

**What Claude noticed:** Losses come mostly from the safety stop, not from the copy rule. 36 of the 48 losers ended at the stop, at about -1.06% each, for roughly -38% in total. The 37 rule exits made +0.39% on average, about +14% in total. Many stop-outs came fast: 21 of the 36 hit within 25 days, some within 0-3 days. The size of the funds' bet does not split winners from losers. 14 of 25 winners and 23 of 48 losers had a net bet under 15%, so raising the entry level would not help.

**Change:**

- `forex.stop_atr_mult`: 3.0 → 4.5 (Safety stop this many typical daily swings (ATR) from the entry)

Study months: Nov 1, 2018 to Aug 20, 2023. Unseen months: Aug 20, 2023 to Nov 1, 2026. Each replay starts with $100.00, fees and price slip included.
