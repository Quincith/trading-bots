**Idea:** Sell when the hedge funds' net bet shrinks to 5% instead of waiting until it fully flips to the other side.

**Why:** Right now we stay in until the hedge funds have completely changed their minds. By then the price has often already turned against us and hit our safety stop. Leaving when their bet gets small means we exit while they are losing interest. That should cut many slow losers early. Good trades usually pay off while the bet is still big, so they should be little affected.

**What Claude noticed:** Losses come from trades that sit too long after the funds' bet has faded. The 36 stop-hit trades averaged -1.06%, and 23 of them were held more than 20 days (e.g. 626, 165, 106, 102, 87 days). The 37 rule exits averaged +0.39%. Several long rule exits that waited for a full flip also lost: 170 days -0.72%, 128 days -1.30%, 189 days -0.26%, 112 days -0.68%. Most winners had quick, clean moves; 15 of 25 were held 50 days or less.

**Change:**

- `forex.exit_net_pct`: 0 → 5.0 (Get out when their net bet falls to this % (0 = it flipped sides))
