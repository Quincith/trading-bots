# Lab experiment 20261115-fxcopy-3: Fxcopy

**❌ Rejected: Better on the unseen months; Steady month to month; Still better with double fees and slip; Not a lucky number; Beats the yardstick (do nothing (keep the cash))**

**Idea:** Only copy the hedge funds when their net bet is at least 15% of all contracts, not 10%.

**Why:** A small net bet means the big funds are not very sure. The price often wobbles against us and hits the safety stop. Waiting for a clearer, bigger bet skips many of these coin-flip trades. The trades we give up were mostly small losers and a few tiny winners. This is a round number, not a finely tuned one.

**What Claude noticed:** Weak bets lose more money. 37 trades had a net bet under 15% (either side). 23 of them lost, together about -21.2%. The 14 winners made only about +8.8%. Most of those losers hit the safety stop at about -1% each. The other 36 trades (15% or more) netted roughly +5%. So the weak-bet trades were the main money drain.

**Change:**

- `forex.min_net_pct`: 10 → 15.0 (Copy when the hedge funds' net bet is at least this % of all open contracts)

Study months: Nov 1, 2018 to Aug 20, 2023. Unseen months: Aug 20, 2023 to Nov 1, 2026. Each replay starts with $100.00, fees and price slip included.

## Checks

- ❌ **Better on the unseen months**: -9.7% with the change vs -7.9% now (needs at least 1 point better)
- ❌ **Steady month to month**: not worse in 2 of 5 slices (needs 4)
- ❌ **Still better with double fees and slip**: -16.8% vs -15.9% with costs doubled
- ❌ **Not a lucky number**: nudged one step either way: -7.6%, -9.0% vs -7.9% now
- ❌ **Beats the yardstick (do nothing (keep the cash))**: -9.7% vs +0.0% on the unseen months
- ✅ **Enough trades to judge**: 29 trades on the unseen months (needs 12)
- ✅ **No deeper losing streak**: worst drop -13.7% vs -13.2% now
- ✅ **Also not worse on the study months**: -0.8% vs -7.4% on the months Claude looked at

_Heads-up: 2 earlier idea(s) were tested on mostly the same unseen months. The more ideas we try on the same months, the likelier one passes by luck, so a kept change still has to prove itself on paper before any real money._
