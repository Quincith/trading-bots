# Lab experiment 20261122-forex-3: Forex

**❌ Rejected: Not a lucky number**

**Idea:** Skip any day where trading fees would eat more than 7.5% of the amount we risk, instead of the current 10%.

**Why:** Every trade pays fees, win or lose. On days when the planned stop is very close, or prices are jumpy and costs are high, fees take a big bite before the trade can work. Many of our losses were tiny flat trades made red by fees, or stops that cost extra in rough weeks. Skipping only the most expensive days should cut mostly those losers. Normal days with good breakouts are kept.

**What Claude noticed:** The file lists 443 losers but only 1 winner, so I cannot compare winners and losers directly. Two things stand out in the losers. First, about 45 'end of session' losers closed between 0.00% and -0.10%. Those trades went nowhere, and fees turned them into losses. Second, stop-hit losses should all be about the same size, near -0.25%. Instead, roughly 50 of them reached -0.40% to -0.73%. These cluster in wild weeks with wide price gaps and higher trading costs: March 2020 (for example -0.60, -0.67, -0.70) and Sep-Oct 2022 and Jan 2023 (-0.54, -0.69, -0.73). Overall, the 366 stop hits averaged -0.32% and the 361 session-end exits averaged +0.34%, so there is a thin edge that costs can easily wipe out. Earlier attempts at a bigger range (60 min) and a later exit (17:00) were both rejected.

**Change:**

- `forex.max_cost_pct`: 10 → 7.5 (Skip days when fees would be more than this % of the risk (0 = off))

Study months: Nov 1, 2018 to Aug 20, 2023. Unseen months: Aug 20, 2023 to Nov 1, 2026. Each replay starts with $100.00, fees and price slip included.
