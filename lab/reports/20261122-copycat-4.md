# Lab experiment 20261122-copycat-4: Copycat

**❌ Rejected: Not a lucky number**

**Idea:** Hold each trade for up to 60 days instead of 30, and keep the same safety exit and profit goal.

**Why:** When company insiders buy their own stock, the payoff often takes months, not weeks. Many of our losers were sold at the deadline while only slightly down. Giving them more time lets slow movers recover or reach the goal. The safety exit still protects us if a stock really falls. Last time, shortening the hold hurt, so trying the opposite direction makes sense.

**What Claude noticed:** Half the losses (13 of 26) were not real failures. They were trades sold at the 30-day time limit with small losses averaging -2.8%, and none of them was near the -10% safety exit. The other 12 time-limit trades were winners averaging +6.2%. Most of these stocks were drifting rather than collapsing, so the clock forced a sale before the idea had played out. Other columns gave no clean split. Losers with 1 insider: 5 vs 6 winners. Score averages were similar. Chase above 5%: 4 losers vs 3 bigger winners. Tightening any of these would have cut more winning dollars than losing ones. Also, the rejected 20-day test did worse on unseen months (-4.9% vs +4.8%), which hints that more time, not less, helps.

**Change:**

- `exits.max_hold_hours`: 720 → 1440 (Sell after this many hours (24 per day) no matter what)
